# How Inflation Actually Destroys Your Savings

**1: Let's First Learn: What Is Inflation?**

> **The Simple Idea**

Inflation means prices increase over time. When prices go up, the same amount of money buys fewer things. Basically, **the value of your money is decreasing.**

**A Little Example:**

| Year | Money | Purchasing Power (What you buy) | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2020 | $100 | 10 Items | Full Power |
| 2025 | $100 | 6 Items | **Weakened** |

---

**2: The Hidden Problem: Money Losing Value**

"If your money just sits in cash, it slowly becomes weaker."

We call this **Purchasing Power Loss**. Nobody is physically stealing your money from your pocket, but the market is stealing its "strength."

For example, your $100 cash today might only have the "buying strength" of $50 in a few years.

---

**3: In Real Life (IRL) Example: The Kebab Index**

Look at how the price of a single meal changes over a decade. This is the most honest way to see inflation:

* **In 2015 Kebab Price:** 25₺
* **In 2025 Kebab Price:** 700₺

**The "Invisible Thief" Table (At 3% Annual Inflation):**

| Year | Real Value of your $100 |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | $100 |
| 10 | $74 |
| 20 | $55 |
| 30 | $41 |

---

# Especially: Why Do People Buy Gold?

In times of crisis, like the **Great Depression (1929)** or major wars, people stop trusting paper cash and run to **Gold**. [USA - Wall Street Great Depression 1929](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Depression)

**Why Gold?**

* **Limited Supply:** Governments can print unlimited paper money, but they can't "print" gold.
* **Trust:** It has been valuable for 5,000 years.
* **Safety:** When the dollar or lira loses value, gold usually keeps its strength.

---

# The Final Lesson

**The biggest mistake young people make is thinking saving money is enough.**

In reality, if you only save cash and never invest (in gold, stocks, or assets), inflation slowly eats your hard work.

> **Don't just save your money. Protect your money.**
